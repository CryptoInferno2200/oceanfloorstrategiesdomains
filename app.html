This device

Install OFS Domains

Pick your device below for the steps.

iPhone + iPad

Add to Home Screen

  1. Open www.ofsdomains.com in Safari. On iOS 16.4 or later, Chrome and Edge work too.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom of the screen (top right on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name OFS Domains and tap Add.

Opened from inside another app? Tap the menu and choose “Open in Safari” first. In-app browsers (Instagram, Facebook, Gmail) can’t install apps.

Android

Install app

  1. Open www.ofsdomains.com in Chrome, Edge, or Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap Install app on this page, or open the browser menu ⋮ and tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Confirm with Install. OFS Domains appears in your app drawer.
Mac, Windows, Chromebook

Desktop app

  1. Chrome or Edge: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or use Install app on this page.
  2. Safari on Mac (Sonoma or later): choose File → Add to Dock.
App Store + Google Play

Store listings

No store download needed. The home-screen app above is the full OFS Domains, with the same account, purchases, and projects. When App Store and Google Play listings go live, links appear here.

Good to know
  • Sign in once inside the app. Your seat, vault, and projects sync from your account.
  • Updates arrive automatically the next time you open the app. Nothing to download again.
  • To remove it, long-press the icon and choose Remove (iPhone) or Uninstall (Android).
