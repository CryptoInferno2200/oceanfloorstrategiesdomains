How the platform works
  1. Describe it. On Home, tell the build desk what you want. When it suggests a build, tap Open build workflow and your brief comes with you to this page.
  2. Check the brief. Edit the brief, business name, logo URL, and description below. A name is suggested from the key words of the brief; you can change it at checkout.
  3. Run the platform. Tap Run the platform. It suggests a name and plan (Claim $10/yr, OFS One $99/yr, or Abyss One $299/month) and sets the listing to web, Web3, or both.
  4. Profile and draft site. Your profile and a draft hosted page are saved. When you are signed in, the project also goes to your account.
  5. Checkout. Checkout opens with your quote. We check availability with the registrar before charging. If your account is at its project limit, Abyss One opens instead.
  6. Registration. After payment, the name is registered at the registrar with WHOIS privacy. Registration status appears in Profile. Haven / Web3 binding is a preview.
