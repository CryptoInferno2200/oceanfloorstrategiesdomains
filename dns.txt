; OFS Domains — zone template (Netlify)
; The site is served by Netlify. Point the apex at Netlify's load balancer and www at the Netlify site.
; If your DNS provider has no ALIAS/ANAME/flattening, use the apex A record Netlify shows in its domain panel.
; Replace APEX with the claimed name (example: yourname.com)
; TTL 300 while you cut over, then 3600.

$TTL 300
@     IN  ALIAS  apex-loadbalancer.netlify.com.
www   IN  CNAME  oceanfloorstrategiesdomains.netlify.app.

; Dual listing. Same letters. Do not invent a new TLD.
_ofs1 IN  TXT    "v=OFS1; listing=both; wallet=haven"
_ens  IN  TXT    "ens-name=APEX"

; Mail. Replace with your own provider's SPF and a role inbox you control.
@     IN  TXT    "v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all"
_dmarc IN TXT    "v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:hostmaster@ofsdomains.com"

; TLS. Netlify issues and renews the certificate with Let's Encrypt (ACME). No PEM to paste.
; CAA locks who may mint.
@     IN  CAA  0 issue "letsencrypt.org"
@     IN  CAA  0 issuewild "letsencrypt.org"
@     IN  CAA  0 iodef "mailto:hostmaster@ofsdomains.com"

; HTTPS is terminated at Netlify.
