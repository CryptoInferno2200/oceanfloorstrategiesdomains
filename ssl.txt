OFS Domains — TLS

SSL is $0. It is not a cart add-on.

After DNS ALIAS/CNAME is live, the host mints the certificate with ACME.
Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, and GitHub Pages all do this. No CSR. No PEM upload.

What we set:
- CAA    only Let's Encrypt, Google Trust, DigiCert may issue
- HSTS   Strict-Transport-Security, 1 year, includeSubDomains, preload
- HTTPS  http → https 301
- iodef  faults to the operator inbox

  certificate subject = APEX and www.APEX
