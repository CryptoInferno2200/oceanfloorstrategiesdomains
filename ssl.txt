OFS Domains — TLS

SSL is $0. It is not a cart add-on.

After the DNS ALIAS/CNAME records point at Netlify, Netlify mints and renews
the certificate with Let's Encrypt (ACME). No CSR. No PEM upload.

What we set:
- CAA    only Let's Encrypt may issue (the authority Netlify uses)
- HSTS   Strict-Transport-Security, 1 year, includeSubDomains, preload
- HTTPS  http → https 301 (Netlify)
- iodef  faults to hostmaster@ofsdomains.com

Check:
  https://APEX
  padlock on Home
  certificate subject = APEX and www.APEX
